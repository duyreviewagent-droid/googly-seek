HOW TO PLAY

Hiders get 30 seconds to hide anywhere in the Giant Bedroom while the seeker counts at the door with their hands over their eyes. Crawl under the bed (press C to duck), stand behind the curtains, sneak into the wardrobe, the tent, the box fort, the dollhouse or the laundry pile — or climb the book stairs onto the bed and the bookshelf.

Seekers hunt everyone down. Get close and click to tag. Every hider you find turns into a seeker and helps you look. Every 30 seconds, all the hiders squeak — listen for it!

Hiders win if anyone is still hidden when the clock runs out. Seekers win if they find everyone. Brave hiders can press F to taunt (+5 coins, but they'll hear you…).

Coins buy skins and pets in the shop. Pets follow you everywhere — even into your hiding spot, so choose wisely.
